The Lenovo Limited Hardware Warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use during the specified warranty period.
The warranty ensures your hardware is free from manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
Coverage applies only when the product is used under "normal use" conditions.
Standard hardware warranties typically do not cover accidental damage (e.g., drops, spills, electrical surges) unless Accidental Damage Protection is purchased.
The warranty period begins on the original date of purchase as shown on your sales receipt or invoice.
Specific components, such as batteries, stylus pens, or digitizer pens, may have different or limited warranty periods (often 12 months).
If your product fails to function as warranted, you should contact Lenovo or an authorized Lenovo Service Provider.
The warranty is generally valid only in the country or region where the product was originally purchased.
This warranty replaces all other express or implied warranties or conditions, including those of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.
