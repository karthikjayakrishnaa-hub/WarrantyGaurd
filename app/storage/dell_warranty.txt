Dell’s Limited Hardware Warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship for Dell-branded hardware products under normal use conditions.
Dell will, at its option, repair or replace hardware that proves defective in materials or workmanship.
The warranty typically excludes normal wear and tear, and damage resulting from accidents, spills, drops, or misuse.
The warranty excludes software, data loss, or issues caused by non-Dell-provided products.
For most standard portable configurations, batteries carry a base 1-year limited warranty, regardless of the system's overall warranty length.
The duration is specified on your original packing slip, invoice, or receipt, which serves as proof of eligibility for service.
If Dell provides a replacement part, its warranty is the longer of 90 days or the remainder of the original system's warranty term.
The limited hardware warranty is intended for the original purchaser and may not be transferable.
Warranty terms, service levels, and durations can vary significantly based on your geographic region and the specific product model.
