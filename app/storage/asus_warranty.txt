ASUS provides a limited hardware warranty for its laptops, which covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions.
The warranty covers technical hardware issues. It does not cover software issues, normal wear and tear, or damage caused by accidents, misuse, improper maintenance.
The warranty generally does not cover damages resulting from external causes such as power surges, accidents, or neglect.
Products with tampered serial numbers or broken warranty seals are not covered.
Repairs performed by non-authorized personnel will void the warranty.
ASUS may choose to repair or replace the product or its components at their discretion. Repaired parts are covered for the remainder of the original warranty period.
You are responsible for backing up your data and removing any passwords before sending the device for service.
Internal laptop batteries often have specific coverage terms and are considered consumables.
Services must be performed by an ASUS-authorized repair center.
