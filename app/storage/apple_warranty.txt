Apple’s Limited Warranty for MacBooks provides coverage against defects in materials and workmanship.
The warranty covers failures due to defects in materials and workmanship when the product is used in accordance with Apple’s published guidelines.
The warranty covers battery issues if the battery degrades below a certain percentage (often 80%) of its original capacity within the warranty period.
Apple-Branded Accessories contained in the original packaging are typically covered under the same terms as the Mac itself.
Apple’s Limited Warranty does not apply to accidental damage, including cracked screens, liquid spills, dents, or damage caused by drops.
Cosmetic damage (scratches, scuffs) and expected battery degradation over time are not considered defects.
Issues caused by unauthorized repairs, third-party software, or the use of non-Apple components or accessories are excluded.
Products not branded as Apple or Beats are not covered, even if sold alongside a MacBook.
You can obtain service through Apple-authorized service providers, the Genius Bar, or by mail-in service.
