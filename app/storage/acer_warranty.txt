Acer's limited warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship for hardware under normal use.
Accidental damage, cosmetic damage, and liquid damage are not covered under standard warranty terms.
The laptop battery is warranted for 1 year from the date of purchase, irrespective of the system warranty.
Warranty claims require the original proof of purchase and serial number validation.
Unauthorized third-party repairs and unauthorized parts may void the warranty.
